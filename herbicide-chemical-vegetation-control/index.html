Herbicide and Chemical Vegetation Control

As Alabama-licensed commercial herbicide applicators, we use targeted treatment when repeated cutting will not solve the vegetation problem. The right method depends on the plant, location and time of year.

We identify the target vegetation and define the treatment area before work begins. We also review nearby water, desirable plants and other sensitive features. Plant growth stage and seasonal conditions help determine the timing.

Applications may be completed with backpack sprayers, vehicle-mounted systems or other methods suited to the property. Treatment can range from individual plants to larger mapped areas. We can also provide treatment records, GIS mapping and follow-up monitoring.

What the Client Can Expect

Targeted field treatment within clearly defined areas, supported by treatment records and mapping when appropriate.
