About the demonstration property

Pine Ridge Demonstration Tract

The portal demonstration uses a fictional 1,240-acre property to show a fully active management program. The acreage belongs only to this example and does not represent SHC's total managed land.

It includes six management types, active fieldwork, wildlife monitoring, timber information, a five-year plan, and annual budget tracking.
